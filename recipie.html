Instructions

  1. Proof the Yeast: In a large bowl or stand mixer bowl, combine the warm milk, 1 teaspoon of granulated sugar, and the yeast. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. Make the Dough: Add the remaining granulated sugar, melted butter, salt, eggs, and about 4 ½ cups of the flour to the yeast mixture. Mix with a dough hook attachment (or stir with a wooden spoon) until a dough forms.
  3. Knead: Knead the dough for 5–8 minutes until it is smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky. If it is too sticky, add the remaining flour a little at a time.
  4. First Rise: Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a warm towel or plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm spot for about 1 to 1.5 hours, or until doubled in size.
  5. Roll and Fill: Punch down the dough and turn it out onto a lightly floured surface. Roll it out into a large rectangle (roughly 12x18 inches). Spread the softened filling butter evenly over the dough, leaving a small border. In a small bowl, mix the brown sugar and cinnamon, then sprinkle it evenly over the butter, pressing it down gently.
  6. Slice and Shape: Roll the dough up tightly from the long edge into a log, then cut it into 12 equal slices using a sharp knife or unflavored dental floss. Place the rolls in a greased 9x13-inch baking pan.
  7. Second Rise: Cover the pan and let the rolls rise a second time for 30 to 45 minutes until puffy.
  8. Bake: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Bake the rolls for 20 to 25 minutes until they are golden brown on top.
  9. Frost: While the rolls cool slightly, beat the cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar, and vanilla together until smooth. Spread or drizzle the frosting over the warm rolls.
